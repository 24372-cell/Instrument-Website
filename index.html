Classical

Classical music spans centuries of western art tradition focused on structured notation and acoustic orchestration. It's renowned for its formal complexity, emotional depth, and precise written notation. Unlike folk or popular music traditions, classical compositions are meticulously structured by composers and brought to life by skilled instrumentalists, choirs, and grands symphony orchestras.

Contemporary

Contemporary music encompasses dynamic modern genre (like pop, rock, and electronic) that favours digital production, flexible structure and broad cultural reach. Breaking away from the rigis structural conventions of traditional classical music, contemporary composers explore new sonic territories through avant-garde techniques, electronic production, minimalism, and atypical instrumentation.
